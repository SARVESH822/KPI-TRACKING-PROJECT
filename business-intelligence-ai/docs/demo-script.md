# Demo Script — Hero Scenario

**Duration target:** 2:30–3:30

**Setup:** `streamlit run app/main.py`, browser open to the app.

---

**0:00–0:20 — Problem**
"Traditional dashboards tell us what happened. They don't tell us why. Finding
out why still takes an analyst days — pulling structured data, cross-checking
tickets and notes, and connecting the dots by hand. We built a KPI storytelling
engine that does this automatically, with evidence, not guesses."

**0:20–0:40 — Show the dashboard**
Point out the Executive Overview: Revenue, Orders, Conversion Rate, Gross
Margin %, each with current value and change vs. the prior period.
"Revenue is down 8% region-wide in August. Let's find out why."

**0:40–1:10 — Select scope and generate**
Select KPI = Revenue, Region = South India, Category = Electronics,
Period = August 2026 vs July 2026. Click **Generate Story**.
"One click — the system runs KPI computation, contribution analysis, and
evidence retrieval, then narrates the result."

**1:10–1:40 — KPI story**
Show the "Why did it change?" tab: executive summary, what changed (exact
numbers), why it changed (ranked drivers).

**1:40–2:10 — Drivers**
Switch to Top Drivers tab. Show the contribution table/chart:
"South India Electronics accounts for the largest share of the decline —
and these contributions are calculated arithmetic, not an LLM guess."

**2:10–2:40 — Evidence**
Switch to Supporting Evidence tab. Show the delivery-delay hypothesis with
its confidence pill and the actual support tickets / ops notes retrieved.
"31 delivery-delay tickets this period vs 13 last period — a real, retrieved
signal, not a hallucinated one."

**2:40–3:10 — Recommendations**
Switch to Recommended Actions tab. Read the top action, its reason, evidence,
and confidence level.
"Every recommendation traces back to a number and a document — nothing here
is invented."

**3:10–3:30 — Differentiation & close**
"Unlike a chatbot over a CSV, every numeric claim here is computed
deterministically before the AI ever sees it — the AI only narrates. And if
the AI API is unavailable, the system still produces the full story from a
rule-based fallback — it never collapses to an error page."

---

## Reproducibility note
This flow depends only on `data/structured/sales.csv` and
`data/unstructured/documents.csv`, generated by `scripts/generate_data.py`
with a fixed random seed. Re-running the generator reproduces the same
hero scenario every time.
